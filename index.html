Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our cabinet installation and closet design services in Atlanta.

How much does a custom kitchen cabinet project cost in Atlanta?

Pricing depends on materials, finishes, and kitchen size. We provide free, no-obligation estimates after an initial consultation. Most kitchen cabinet projects in Atlanta range from mid-tier to premium budgets.

Do you serve areas outside of Atlanta?

Yes. We work across Atlanta, Cumming, Alpharetta, Roswell, Johns Creek, and Sandy Springs. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific address.

How long does a kitchen cabinet installation take?

A typical kitchen cabinet installation takes 1–3 weeks depending on the scope, custom elements, and finishing details. We provide a clear timeline before the project starts.

What cabinet styles do you offer?

Modern, shaker, luxury, European, and contemporary styles. We can match any aesthetic — from minimalist clean lines to refined timeless designs.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, all initial estimates are free. We discuss your vision, take measurements, and provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Are walk-in closet systems customizable?

Absolutely. Every closet system is designed around your wardrobe, lifestyle, and space — including double-hang zones, shoe storage, drawers, and shelving.

Do you handle both design and installation?

Yes. We manage the full process — design, materials, manufacturing coordination, and professional installation — so you have a single point of contact.

What materials do you use?

Premium-grade plywood boxes, solid wood doors, soft-close hardware, and durable finishes. Material options are discussed during the estimate.
